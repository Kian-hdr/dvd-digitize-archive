# Blu-ray and UHD decisions

Read this reference only for Blu-ray media. The shared workspace, verification, and confirmation gates remain in `SKILL.md`.

## Identify the source

- A mounted video Blu-ray normally exposes `BDMV/index.bdmv`, `BDMV/PLAYLIST/*.mpls`, `BDMV/CLIPINF/*.clpi`, and `BDMV/STREAM/*.m2ts`. Run `scripts/disc_snapshot.py` for a repeatable structural fingerprint and file inventory. Also retain MakeMKV's current drive scan and disc info as separate evidence. The fingerprint is not a unique physical-disc serial number.
- Check the optical drive and tool against the actual disc. UHD support is drive/firmware/tool dependent; do not update drive firmware or acquire a key, license, or tool on speculation. If the disc is inaccessible, record the exact error and stop that acquisition.
- Estimate required workspace free space from selected title sizes plus temporary backup and playback copies. A Blu-ray/UHD may require far more space than a DVD. Recheck free space before a full-disc backup or transcode.

## Select titles and acquire

Use `scripts/makemkv_job.py` for the current `scan` and `info` operations, then preserve the raw output in disc-specific logs. Its [CLI reference](https://www.makemkv.com/developers/usage.txt) documents `info`, `mkv`, `backup`, `--robot`, and progress records. Confirm the current drive index from the scan; never infer it from a device number. Record the exact selected MakeMKV title ID, playlist ID when available, size, duration, segment/angle map, chapters, audio and subtitle streams, and selection reason before extraction. Do not select `all` as a shortcut when extras, alternate cuts, or duplicate playlists are unresolved.

For a film, compare candidate playlists and actual scene order. Seamless branching, alternate cuts, angles, and intentionally similar playlists can share durations. For episodic discs, corroborate each episode's content/order with chapters, visible opening/end material, packaging, and reliable episode sources. Preserve uncertain candidates under factual provisional names; do not assign an episode from playlist number or runtime alone.

After the physical-disc confirmation gate, preview then run one explicit `title` job per established title to a distinct `.work/` directory. Before extraction, verify that **every audio track** on the chosen title is selected, including all language dubs, commentary, descriptive audio and alternate mixes; MakeMKV's default track selection is not proof that none were omitted. Preserve MakeMKV's exit status and log. Its [online help](https://www.makemkv.com/onlinehelp/) describes per-title and per-track selection. If playlist choice cannot be settled from the live disc, a separately named temporary `backup` job may be useful; MakeMKV's backup operation is for Blu-ray/UHD, and a title MKV does not retain menus or every playlist. Verify backup readability and structure before treating it as a reusable local source. Do not claim that a filesystem copy of encrypted `BDMV` streams is a usable source.

When complete-disc coverage is requested, preserve a decrypted full-disc backup before releasing the physical disc, then verify the backup's `BDMV` structure, media-file inventory and MakeMKV title readability. Use `makemkv_job.py info --backup-dir VERIFIED_FOLDER` and selected `title --backup-dir VERIFIED_FOLDER` jobs for local extraction. The helper uses `--minlength=0` for both info and selected titles so short bonus/menu-video title IDs do not shift or disappear. Inspect every listed title and any media streams not represented by those titles; record exact duplicate playlists before deduplicating. An MP4 cannot reproduce interactive menu navigation, so retain its backup representation until the collection's menu requirement is resolved.

If MakeMKV's local `file:` reader fails or crashes despite a complete backup, record the exact failure once and verify decrypted `.m2ts` readability with FFprobe and representative full FFmpeg decodes. Use explicit stream-copy remuxes of the verified local media files to distinct working MKVs, preserving all video/audio/PGS streams that the containers support. Compare every resulting stream inventory with the raw backup and disc info; stop at language, playlist or cut ambiguity rather than repeatedly retrying a crashing reader.

## Preserve technical features

- Record source codec, resolution, exact frame rate, scan mode, color primaries/transfer/matrix, bit depth, and HDR/Dolby Vision metadata when present. Do not convert 23.976 to 24 or deinterlace based solely on a header. Keep the original-stream working MKV until the final MP4's feature choices and checks are resolved.
- Retain every source audio track in the original-stream MKV until the user’s language and preservation policy is settled. For an English/German MP4 profile, choose a verified primary mix in each available language, preserving the source channel count, and retain requested original bitstreams as verified `.mka` sidecars or in the MKV. Other language combinations require explicit helper options and verification. Do not infer that a stereo fallback, commentary or copied original belongs as an extra selectable MP4 track. Keep full immersive originals rather than only a lower-fidelity core; ordinary 5.1 channel count does not prove Atmos.
- Keep Blu-ray PGS bitmap subtitles, including forced or signs/songs variants, in the temporary source while needed. Verify actual flags and content; do not infer forced status from a title string. [HandBrake's subtitle documentation](https://handbrake.fr/docs/en/latest/advanced/subtitles.html) confirms PGS cannot pass through to MP4. Use reviewed OCR for requested selectable MP4 text; leave the source in place if required subtitles are unresolved.
- Treat 3D MVC and UHD HDR as special cases. Do not claim one standard H.264 SDR MP4 preserved 3D/HDR. Choose and verify a suitable MP4 target or keep the working source pending a user decision. [HandBrake's filter reference](https://handbrake.fr/docs/en/latest/technical/filters-summary.html) documents colorspace/tone mapping as a conversion.

## Verification additions

Compare the final MP4's scene order and chapter boundaries with the selected playlist and verified episode map. Sample every branch, angle change, playlist join, episode opening, middle, and ending. Confirm no missing, repeated, or reordered scene and no audio/subtitle drift at segment joins. Fully decode each final video/audio stream with `scripts/media_audit.py --decode`, inspect warnings, and test the actual TV route. Verify source features the MP4 is expected to preserve with a capable player. Keep the exact tool/version, title/playlist mapping, source limitations, and verification evidence in the disc log.

For source access, VideoLAN notes that [libbluray parses navigation and playlists but does not itself handle commercial disc protection](https://images.videolan.org/developers/libbluray.html). Do not treat successful playlist parsing as proof that protected streams were acquired or decoded.
